Help with your AgentKey account, device vault and encrypted handoffs.

Sign in and unlock

Apple or Google sign-in opens your AgentKey account. Device authentication opens its local encrypted vault. These are separate protections. Use the same AgentKey account on each device.

Save a login

Open Vault, add a login, and enter its name, username, password and exact HTTPS website. The website is the origin, without a path or query, such as https://example.com.

Agent contacts

Adding a verified agent contact does not grant it access to your saved logins. Contact photos stay only on the device where you add them. Automatic credential execution and shared vaults are not included in this version.

AgentKey in Messages

Unlock the main AgentKey app before opening its Messages extension. Save a login there to create an encrypted handoff. The conversation receives a receipt, not the password. Return to the main app and unlock it to import the login.

Backup and recovery

Encrypted backup is opt-in. Its recovery key is stored separately in iCloud Keychain. You need the same Apple Account with Keychain enabled and the same AgentKey account on the other device. Account sign-in alone cannot recover a lost encryption key. Keep another safe copy until you have tested recovery on a second device.

Account and privacy requests

The maintained support contact is being verified before this page is released. Do not send passwords, recovery keys or verification codes by email.
