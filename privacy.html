Privacy, in plain words.

What AgentKey stores, what stays on your device, and what happens when you choose to share or back up.

Release draft. Service retention and deletion availability are still being verified. This page is not the published privacy policy.

Saved logins

Your usernames, passwords, websites and notes are stored in an encrypted device vault. Its key is protected by device authentication. If you choose cloud backup, encrypted vault contents and backup identifiers go to AgentKey's service. The encryption key is not sent with that backup. A separate recovery copy may be stored in your iCloud Keychain.

Account and contacts

Apple or Google handles sign-in. AgentKey's service stores your account email and identifier, authentication information, verified agent contact names and identifiers, preferences, and account-linked request and grant history. These support account isolation, contact setup and security. Adding a contact grants no access to saved logins.

Photos

Contact photos stay on your device. AgentKey resizes and re-encodes them to remove image metadata, and excludes them from device backup. It does not send them to agents or its service.

Messages and sharing

The Messages extension captures a login into an encrypted local handoff. The conversation receives a receipt, not the captured username or password. For a human share you explicitly choose, the service holds the encrypted share plus sender, recipient, website, item version and expiry metadata. A recipient who imports it holds a separate copy; later revocation cannot erase that copy.

Service providers

Supabase provides authentication, database and service hosting. Apple and Google provide their sign-in processes; Apple provides iCloud Keychain. No advertising or cross-app tracking is implemented in AgentKey. The public support site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process connection information needed to serve it.

Account deletion

The release's account-owner-confirmed deletion flow is being tested before it becomes available. Its intended scope is account-linked service data, cloud vault ciphertext, this account's device vault and recovery keys. Other accounts, earlier device-only vaults, imported recipient copies, physical backups and offline-device copies are separate. Historical Apple sign-in tokens may need separate removal from Sign in with Apple.

Contact

Email agentkey@waiwiser.com for help with your account or a privacy request. Never email your vault passwords or recovery keys.
